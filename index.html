Meet the Cook

Gladys Gatimu is the heart behind every plate

Gladys Gatimu started Gladys Kitchen with one simple idea: everyone in Nanyuki deserves a hot, delicious meal without breaking the bank. Every chapati is rolled by hand, every piece of chicken is fried to golden perfection, and every plate of chips kuku is served with the kind of care you'd expect from family.

Customers keep coming back not just for the food, but for the warmth — Gladys knows many of her regulars by name, and treats every new face like a friend she hasn't met yet. That's the Gladys Kitchen difference: honest food, honest prices, made with real love.
